Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Investment Analysis

Project Overview

This project presents a quantitative investment analysis of Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) using Python. The analysis examines Alphabet’s historical market performance and evaluates how its performance and risk characteristics have changed during 2026.

The project combines financial market data, statistical analysis, risk metrics, technical analysis, CAPM and Monte Carlo simulation to provide a structured assessment of Alphabet’s investment performance.

Objectives

The analysis seeks to answer five key questions:

1. How has Alphabet performed historically?
2. What level of risk has investors had to accept to achieve those returns?
3. How does Alphabet’s risk and return profile compare with the broader market?
4. How has Alphabet performed in 2026 relative to its historical performance?
5. What does the analysis suggest about Alphabet’s current investment profile?

Analysis Covered

The notebook covers the following areas:

* Price and market performance
* Historical returns
* Annualized return
* Volatility
* Risk-adjusted performance
* Drawdown analysis
* Correlation with the S&P 500
* Beta and systematic risk
* CAPM and required return
* Moving averages and technical analysis
* 2026 performance review
* Monte Carlo simulation

Data and Methodology

Data Source

Market data was obtained from Yahoo Finance.

Benchmark

The S&P 500 Index (^GSPC) is used as the market benchmark.

Analysis Periods

Historical period: January 2019 – December 2025

2026 measurement period: January 1, 2026 – August 26, 2026

Risk-Free Rate

A risk-free rate of 4.64%, based on the U.S. 10-Year Treasury yield as of August 26, 2026, was used in the CAPM and risk-adjusted performance analysis.

Monte Carlo Simulation

The Monte Carlo analysis uses:

* 10,000 simulations
* 252 trading days
* One-year simulation horizon

Key Findings

Alphabet generated an annualized historical return of approximately 30.55% between 2019 and 2025, with annualized volatility of approximately 31%.

The company’s strongest annual performance during the historical period occurred in 2025, when Alphabet returned approximately 65.99%. Its weakest year was 2022, when the stock declined approximately 39.08%.

Alphabet’s historical correlation with the S&P 500 was approximately 72%, while its beta of approximately 1.13 indicates that the stock has historically exhibited slightly greater sensitivity to movements in the broader market.

In 2026, Alphabet’s performance has been considerably weaker than its historical average. From January 1 to August 18, 2026, the stock generated an approximate return of 8.66%, compared with its historical annualized return of approximately 30.55%.

The analysis also examines Alphabet’s 2026 drawdown, volatility, risk-adjusted performance and technical positioning to assess whether the weaker return represents a deterioration in the company’s investment profile or primarily reflects changing market conditions and expectations.
